About

I'm a 3D generalist from Buenos Aires with a degree in Multimedia Design from ISEC. What pulls me into 3D is building worlds: scenes where the lighting, the materials and the small details tell a story before anyone explains it.

I've worked as Lighting & Render Director on the short film Astros de Papel, as a freelance 3D artist, and as a web and graphic designer, which gave me a strong eye for composition and a habit of working closely with teams.

Buenos Aires, Argentina · Spanish native · English advanced
